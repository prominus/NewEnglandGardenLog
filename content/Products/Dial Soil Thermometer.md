### Instructions
Insert the thermometer to a maximum depth of 2" or 3" in the soil. Check the recorded temperature after 3 minutes.

### Features
- Easy to read dial in F° scaled from 30° to 90°
- Stainless steel probe with glass lens
- 6.5" temperature probe
