The schematic showed 3 [[Montana Zemo Hardneck Garlic]] left on the ground, but I pulled out over 6 Hardneck bulbs from the ground. I suspecting mixed variety here, so I will not use these particular bulbs to replant. 

![[20260724_114338.webp|600]]

I'm on the fence if I will replant [[Silver White Softneck Garlic]]. The bulbs were small and the stalks loved to snap off as I pulled them out of the ground. The flavor will have to be a show stopper to convince me to replant.
