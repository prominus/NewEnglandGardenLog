### Description

- Popular early potato
- Yellow-fleshed with rich flavor
- Thin, gold skins with shallow eyes
- Produces high yields
- Stores well
- Certified seed potatoes

Treasured by gardeners for decades, Yukon Gold Potato is a popular early-season potato that produces a big, great-tasting crop of yellow-fleshed spuds with thin, gold skins and shallow eyes. Its moist flesh and sweet, buttery flavor make it a favorite for boiling, baking, frying, mashing and made into potato salads. The plants produce plenty of spuds that can be harvested at the new potato and mature stages. This sprout-resistant potato stores well too, so you can enjoy richly flavored spuds for months.
### Variety Info

**Days to Maturity:** 60 - 80 Days

**Family:** Solanaceae

**Scientific Name:** Solanum tuberosum

**Hardiness:** 3-9

**Exposure:** Full sun

**Plant Dimensions:** 24 - 36 inches. 25 inches wide

**Variety Info:**   
Small white flowers. Golden-yellow fleshed, smooth yellow skin with shallow eyes. Gourmet flavor.

### Sowing Info

**When to Sow Outside:** Typically planted 2 to 4 weeks before the average last frost date in the spring

**Seed Depth:** 3 to 5 inches.

**Seed Spacing:** 12 to 15 inches

**Row Spacing:** 30 to 36 inches

### Growing Info

**Harvesting:** 2 lbs. of seed potatoes will make about 10 - 15 potato sets when cut and will plant 10 - 15 feet of row.
