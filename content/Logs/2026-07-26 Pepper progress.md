### Other notes

- The [[Gherkin Hokus]] leaves are drying out and turning yellow. I have a feeling they are being overtaken by a virus passed by the cucumber beetles I've seen on the plants. Regardless, I intend to replace these with [[Pickling Cucumber]] next year. On the other end -
- The [[Marketmore]] plants are going gangbusters.  I already pulled 6 cucumbers and already see more coming in.
- One of the [[Triumph Zucchini]] plants hit by the [[squash bug]] looks pretty dead. Definitely need to cover next season.
