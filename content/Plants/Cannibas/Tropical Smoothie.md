#### Description

Tropical Smoothie leads with bright mango and pineapple on the nose, backed by lemon and a thread of pine that keeps it from going too sweet. The effects land squarely in the head — alert, focused, and creatively productive without any restlessness. Limonene and beta-caryophyllene drive the terpene profile, with myrcene, alpha-humulene, and linalool filling in the edges. A true sativa that earns the label

**Main Effects:** True sativa, Energizing, creative

**Flavor:** Mango, Pineapple, Lemon, Pine

**Lineage:** Citrus smoothie, Velvet pie

**THC:** 25%-30%

**Total Cannabinoids:** 27% - 36%

**Dominant Terpenes:** Myrcene, Limonene, Bisabolol F1 Hybrid, feminized 99.6% stable

**Days to finish:** 56
