All squash fall under the Cucurbita genus.  The main species of cucurbits are:
- **pepo**: acorn squash, delicata, summer squash, [[Dark Zucchini]], [[Triumph Zucchini]], [[Scallop Blend Patty Pan]]
- **maxima**: buttercup, Hubbard, kabocha, giant pumpkins, [[Lakota Winter Squash]]
- **moschata**: butternut, most pumpkins, [[Honeynut Winter Squash]]

Cucurbita moschata is generally the easiest to grow. Cucurbita maxima are generally the hardest.
