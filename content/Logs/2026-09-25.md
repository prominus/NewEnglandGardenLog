Threw down more of the [[Crimson Clover]] in the [[City Community Garden]] between bare patches were peppers and other plants have been pulled. I did not realize this, but the description notes that it is a cover crop best used in spring or fall in cool weather. Given how cool the weather has been maybe it will do better than when I scattered some in the summer.
