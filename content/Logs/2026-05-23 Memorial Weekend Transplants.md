## Day 2 of the transplants

On Saturday (2026-05-23) I continued transplant secondary plants such as [[Tarragon]], [[Italian Genovese Basil]], [[Finocchio Fennel]], [[King Henry Viola]]s, and [[Gem Blend Signet Marigold]]s.  I also scattered [[Contender Bush Beans]], [[Mardi Gras Beans]], and old herb seeds such as cilantro and basil.  I direct seed [[Honeynut Winter Squash]], [[Gherkin Hokus]], and a couple [[Glass Gem Flint]] corn where some of the transplant corn seemed to fail.

A few of the pepper plants have fairly weak stems. Specifically the [[Thai Dragon Peppers]], [[Shishito Chile Peppers]], and a couple of the Ajis. I used foot long fiberglass posts and twine to provide these peppers support.  I also placed support for the one [[Shishito Chile Peppers]] on the west most part of bed 4. It's bushing incredibly low and a few of the branches were on the ground. I pruned a few branches that were too long.
