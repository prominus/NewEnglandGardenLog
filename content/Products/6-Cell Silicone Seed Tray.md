### Description

Our original 6-cell tray is our most compact and economical tray, featuring large 2” cells, drain holes, and thick silicone walls. This tray can grow almost anything -- a loyal asset to any gardener!

**Made from 100% Silicone, BPA-Free, Food-Grade:** Safety is our priority. Our trays are made from 100% food-grade, BPA-free silicone, ensuring they are safe for use with all types of plants, including edible crops.

**Reuse Every Season**: Our silicone seed trays are designed for long-term use, so you can rely on them season after season.

**Easy Soil Release:** The flexible silicone construction allows for effortless soil release, ensuring seedlings come out without damage or hassle. No more struggling with hard-to-remove plants!

**Heat and UV Resistant:** Whether you grow in the arctic winter or desert summer, Sili-Seedlings can withstand the rigors of frequent use in any environment.

**High Flood Rim:** Preventing water spillage over the sides, our flood rims make watering easy and consistent.

**Drain Holes:** Each cell features large drain holes to promote water drainage, preventing waterlogged roots and encouraging healthy root development for your seedlings.

**Fit Seven Trays in a Standard 1020 Bottom Tray (Not Included):** You can fit up to eight if you don’t mind them a little squeezed!

**World’s First Silicone Seed Tray**: Created in 2017, this 6-cell tray was the first seed tray made of silicone.
