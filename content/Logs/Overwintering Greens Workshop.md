# Synopsis

**Workshop:** Overwintering Greens

**Date:** Thursday, September 10

**Time:** 5:30-7pm

**Location:** Greenhouse at Landry Park, 62 Pine Street, Winooski. Please see the attached map for details on where to park and how to find the greenhouse.

# Notes

- Lookup more info on the [[Persephone Period]]
- Getting things ready before [[Persephone Period]] hits
- There are two dates to hit for harvesting:
	- Set up at start (nowish -> winter solstice)
	- overwinter for spring (winter solstice -> early spring)
- Things to start in August for New England
	- carrots mid August
	- scallions at beginning
- Things to start in September
	- mustard greens around Sept 1st
	- Spinach around mid Sept
- Aim to have plants at 75% maturity @ start of [[Persephone Period]] (~ November 5th)
- As soon snow melts and soil is workable you can plant for the early spring
