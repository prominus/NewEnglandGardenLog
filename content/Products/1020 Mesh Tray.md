# 1020 Mesh Deep Extra Strength Colors - 2.5" Deep

### Description

## USA Made Mesh Deep Trays

These mesh bottom trays are built out of Heavy Duty, BPA, FDA Prime, Polyethylene plastic. Guaranteed not to break or warp even with heavy loads.  

Great for soil block propagation or for anyone that needs a tray with extra drainage. Our soil block trays are built with Extra-Strength so they won't sag, break or warp even while transporting heavy loads. Now available in bright colors! Use a deep [1020 tray](https://www.bootstrapfarmer.com/collections/1020-trays-and-flats/products/1020-trays-multi-color "1020 heavy duty trays") for bottom watering for the perfect system!

### **Features**

- BPA Free, Virgin, Food-Safe #2 PE Plastic
- Mesh holes for ideal drainage
- UV Resistant
- Made in Midwest, USA
- Outer dimensions: 21¼” x 11¼” x 2½” Deep
- Ideal for use with 1020 trays, but strong enough to be used on its own
- We recommend using trays of the same depth when nesting trays.
- Mesh trays are designed to sit inside a no hole tray of the same depth. If you are stacking be aware that the no hole tray will sit about 1/4 inch above the bottom of the mesh tray.
- _**Note**- Store trays out of direct sunlight when not in use. Prolonged exposure to direct sunlight will lead to degradation_

### Ideal Uses

- Wheatgrass Tray
- Fodder Tray
- Soil Block Tray
- Rockwool Cubes

Use our soil block propagation trays for any rockwool cubes, fodder, micro-greens, or wheatgrass. With just enough flat mesh to equally carry all sizes of soil blocks, these trays allow root air pruning on all sides of the blocks.
