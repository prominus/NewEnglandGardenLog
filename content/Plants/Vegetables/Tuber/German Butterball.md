### Description

- Versatile, all-purpose heirloom
- Smooth, golden skin and slightly flaky flesh
- Medium, oblong round potatoes
- Buttery, superior flavor
- Excellent storage qualities
- Certified seed potatoes

**Late.** Rich, golden russet-type potatoes are an all-around favorite for baking, frying and steaming. Truly an all-purpose potato, with delicious, buttery flavor and a tender, flaky texture. Just one look and taste and Butterball will be an instant winner in your kitchen and your garden. Excellent for winter storage.

### Variety Info

**Days to Maturity:** 100 - 130 Days 

**Family:** Solanaceae

**Scientific Name:** Solanum tuberosum

**Hardiness:** 3-9

**Exposure:** Full sun

**Plant Dimensions:** 24 - 36 inches. 25 inches wide

**Variety Info:**   
Flowers are star shaped purple blooms. Russet-type with rich, golden, slightly flaky flesh. Medium, oblong to round potatoes. Smooth golden skin & flesh.

### Sowing Info

**When to Sow Outside:** Typically planted 2 to 4 weeks before the average last frost date in the spring

**Seed Depth:** 3 to 5 inches.

**Seed Spacing:** 12 to 15 inches

**Row Spacing:** 30 to 36 inches

### Growing Info

**Harvesting:** Early Summer, Mid Summer, Late Summer, All Summer.   
Since a single plant will likely produce up to 2 - 4 pounds, the yield from a 10 foot row could be anywhere from 25 - 35 pounds.
